Daily schedule

The recommended shape of each kind of day on your rota — when to sleep, eat, train, and bank your 10,000 steps. It auto-selects today's shift, adapts to where you are in the block, and eases off on red-recovery days. A template, not a rulebook.

The maths behind it

10,000 steps ≈ 100 minutes of walking. On shift: commute walk (~1,700) + three walk breaks (~4,900) gets you past half before counting a single ward lap — 12 hours on your feet usually covers the rest. Off days need the deliberate 45–60 min walk; there's no ward doing the work for you.

Best slots in order: off-day mid-morning (hard sessions), pre-shift on nights (fed + rested), post-shift on days (short and sharp). Match intensity to recovery colour: green = push, yellow = zone 2 / lighter weights, red = mobility only. Never train between a night shift and day sleep.

The levers that make nights survivable: caffeine stops at midnight, sunglasses on the morning commute, one continuous day-sleep block, bright light during the 3–4am dip, and a SHORT sleep (not a full day) after the last night. Light and caffeine timing move your body clock more than willpower does.
